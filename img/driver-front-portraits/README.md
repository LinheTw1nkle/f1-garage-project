# 2026 driver front asset audit

## Portraits

22 official Formula 1 front-facing portraits downloaded as transparent 1000 x 1200 PNG files. These are upper-body crops of the official full-body originals, using the official image CDN crop service. Sources are recorded in sources.json; image dimensions and corner alpha are recorded in validation.json.

## Helmets

No fully front-facing AND transparent-background 2026 helmet image has been verified in this search. Existing img/driver-helmets images are three-quarter views and do not satisfy this requirement.

Verified front-view candidate: Antonelli replica helmet at https://www.headgrid.fr/products/antonelli-2026/5.png . Inspected original is 1254 x 1254 RGB, with a white wall and wooden table background; it is NOT transparent and has not been added as a compliant asset.

Other references checked:
- Audi official 2026 launch helmet gallery: https://contenthub.audif1.com/en/images/albums/2026-launch-helmets-37
- Leclerc 2026 replica helmet gallery: https://www.f1authentics.com/products/charles-leclerc-2026-season-ferrari-1-2-scale-helmet
